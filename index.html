About Me

Architect. Leader. Problem Solver.

I'm a VP-level Enterprise Architect with a track record of transforming complex technology landscapes across two of the most demanding industries — banking and telecommunications.

At Globe Telecom, I oversee flagship programs including FSM (Field Service Management), the C2C centralized catalogue initiative, and a network architecture overhaul that has collectively delivered hundreds of thousands of dollars in annual savings — while meaningfully improving customer experience and operational resilience.

Before Globe, I built Trust Bank's entire cloud-native core platform from scratch as Head of Core Platform — scaling Singapore's first digital bank from zero to 700,000 customers in nine months with six-nines availability, while achieving a 45% reduction in infrastructure spend.

Earlier in my career I led multi-cloud transformation for NAB's top 20 critical banking applications (including Fast Payments with zero downtime), and drove Australia's first fully cloud-native university transformation at the University of Newcastle — migrating 180+ applications and decommissioning the data centre entirely.
